3. Fixable share by plugin version

4. What the follow-ups actually are

Scope literalism is the leak, not mechanism substitution. Comment and doc lag, in-diff defects and vacuous test pins make up 73% of all filings, and in most of those the seat's own "why not absorbable" text is a scope argument ("the slice does not name this file"), not a difficulty argument. 189 of the 333 fixable issues were five lines or fewer.

6. Reading against #1492's boundaries

  • Authored half (window B): 9 issues, too few to read.
  • Doctrine landed (plan 11 phase 2, release 0.17.11 on 08-18): no drop follows. The three amended plans filed 1.7 to 2.8 per task.
  • Full effect vs control: plans 12 and 13 ran at 1.7 each. Control plan 14 ran at 1.1 with one #1431-class issue (#1621) against one in plan 13 and none in plans 11 and 12. No contrast in the predicted direction.
  • #1431's deferred backstop (re-open on a routed issue the clause should have covered) is met by #1774 and #1813 at least. Both bodies say the substitution held the End states and was filed anyway.
  • #1547's batch-revert mode is nearly absent here: one issue. Sweep-never-ran demotions are 52.
  • First visible drop: the two newest plans under 0.21.x ace re-entry filed 0.2 and 0.1 per task.

Method and caveats

  • Numerator: every issue carrying the war-followup label, open and closed. Denominator: ### Task headings in the executed plan file, attributed from the issue body's plan or epic citation. 12 older July issues could not be attributed.
  • Classification: eight agents, one pass each over issue text with light repo lookups. 73% high confidence, 27% medium, 1 low. Treat class counts as plus or minus about 10%.
  • Denominator is planned tasks, not landed tasks. Held phases inflate engine-reliability and in-run-finding-resolution slightly.
  • Measurement confound: window A counts were Lead-filtered by hand. Window C counts come from the engine's consolidated filing dispatch. Part of the jump is instrumentation, not behavior.
  • Closure data: each closed issue's GitHub timeline (closing event, referenced commits, cross-referenced PRs).
