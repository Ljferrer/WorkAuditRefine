- **The living-ADR law (PIN-1 ‡, operator ruling R1).** An ADR body states current law and is rewritten in place; git is the archive; every ADR carries a `## Decision log` (one line per change: date · what flipped · driving issue — and, when the line retires prior text, a trailing `retired: docs/adr/<file> @ <sha>` field, PIN-26 ‡); a new ADR takes the next free number at land time — this plan's two new ADRs are named by slug (`*-living-adrs-git-is-the-archive.md`, `*-visual-surfaces-are-projections.md`) and every validation resolves them by glob, never by number (PIN-24 ‡). ADR 0044's rewrite (Phase 2) and the corpus migration (Phase 4) are written under Phase 1's law — phase edges, not `deps`.
- **A retirement cites the retired text's sha (PIN-26 ‡, operator ruling 2026-09-06).** An ADR sentence that retires prior law cites the retired text's commit sha in the sha-pinned form — `docs/adr/<file> @ <sha>`, the sha of the commit that introduced the retired text — the **oldest** hit of `git log --reverse -S'<retired literal>' --format=%h -- <file> | head -1` (or equivalent), because `-S` returns every commit that touched the literal and only the oldest is the introducing one — and the Decision-log line that records the retirement carries the same sha in its trailing `retired:` field. "Git remembers the rest" is true only while the pointer exists. Binding on the meta-ADR (Task 1.1 states it), ADR 0044's fold (Task 2.4), every fold in the migration (Task 4.1), and every description of the Decision-log line format in this plan.
- **Citation forms (PIN-2).** Three forms, ratified in the meta-ADR: a bare citation (`ADR 0013`) means current law; a dated citation (`ADR 0013 (2026-08-25)`, or the existing living-prose wording `ADR 0013 amendment 2026-08-25` / `amended 2026-08-25`) resolves to that date's Decision-log line; a sha-pinned citation (`docs/adr/<file> @ <sha>`) means the file as it stood at that commit. Consequence: the dated citations already in living prose stay valid after the fold and are not swept; only citations that name a *heading* (`see ADR 0044 Amendment (2026-08-24)`) are rewritten.
- **Policy-lifecycle vs record (PIN-28 ‡; OD-1 ruled 2026-09-06 — PIN-3 retired in place: its pre-land/post-land split and `## Post-land corrections` appendix contradicted the living law this plan lands).** One rule for the whole life of a file, no lifecycle judgment call.
