## Pivotal constraints

- Intent is never agent-invented (ADR 0013). A fork trial settles a fact. It never writes Purpose, Method or an End state.
- Part 2 extraction headings are untouched. Every new Part 1 record is placement latitude, never a new required H2 (ADR 0044).
- New doctrine lands cold: one references file, `when <trigger>, read` pointers on the hot surfaces, byte-identical moves only (ADR 0042).
- Role purity: `/war-strategy` interviews and converts. `/red-team` validates. This plan changes no `/red-team` file (PIN-10).
- Engine untouched: `workflow-template.js`, hooks, floors, schemas' Workflow args, intake. The `interview` config section is never threaded into Workflow args (PIN-11).
- WAR never depends on pstack, `design-an-interface` or any Grill Me skill. They are optional front doors.
- Every pinned surface a task edits re-anchors its pins in the same task: the structure test, D32 rows, the census, the war-machine pipeline test.
- `CONTEXT.md` bytes are funded before they are spent. No task raises the hard line (ADR 0048). Both evictions are byte-identical (ADR 0042 D3).
- Bounds are a safety precaution (ADR 0052 §4). Every trial carries a wall cap, and the interview reads its trial count aloud.
- The one sandbox form is `git clone --no-hardlinks` into the scratchpad. The escape guard runs around every executed trial. The guard is fail-closed on the TRIAL row, never on the interview (PIN-1).
- Launch precondition: `/war` launches only when `git merge-base --is-ancestor 76a8b6fb <working>` exits 0 (End state 14). Any other exit halts at Setup (PIN-4, PIN-6).
- Every claim in this plan was re-measured at the merged master `76a8b6fb` on 2026-10-26 (PIN-4; ledger A2 retired). A later master move before `/red-team` repeats the re-measurement.
