- Purpose: five verified guards currently pass green without exercising what they lock, and two stager fail-loud arms have no guard at all — make every one of them demonstrably RED-able, so a future edit that deletes or flips a guarded fact fails loudly instead of shipping under a vacuous pass or an absent one.
